# Backup and Sync

**Discovered During:** personal-thoughts-maui-app_2026-10-18

## Context
The MVP keeps all data on the device. Losing the phone loses every thought.

## Known Scope
Options: Android Auto Backup rules for the DB and media, a manual export/import archive, or cloud sync (OneDrive or Google Drive).

## Trigger for Promotion
When the app is in daily use and the data is worth protecting.
